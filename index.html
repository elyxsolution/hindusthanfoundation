Every building stands on something you never see.

A pile is a long concrete column cast deep into the ground. It carries the weight of a building down to firm soil or rock, so the structure above never settles, tilts or cracks.

We bore the hole, lower in the steel cage, fill it with concrete, and then prove it with a load test. That is all we do, and we do it every day.

  • Bored cast-in-situ piles

    Rotary-rig boring for towers, villas, tech parks, plants and airport structures.

  • Shoring piles

    Piles up to 1200 mm in diameter that hold back the earth around deep basements.

  • Static load testing

    Kentledge tests up to 750 MT and rock-anchor tests beyond 900 MT prove the design load.
