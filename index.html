Who We Are

We are not just builders; we are the bedrock of modern infrastructure.

Engineering Excellence Since Inception

Hindustan Foundations and Construction specializes in critical groundwork engineering. Unlike general contractors, we focus purely on what lies beneath — ensuring that every structure has a safe, stable, and scientific base.

Our team of geotechnical engineers and planners use state-of-the-art piling technologies to tackle the most challenging terrains.

Our Leadership

PH Krishna Reddy

Proprietor & Founder

With over 30 years of experience in foundation and geotechnical construction, he established Hindustan Foundations and Constructions in 1998.

Under his leadership, the company has successfully executed projects ranging from residential buildings to large-scale tech parks. His expertise further extends to defence infrastructure and complex pile foundation works, notably contributing to the development of Terminals 1 and 2 at Bangalore International Airport (BIAL).

Vishruth Krishna Reddy

Director – Operations & Project Management

A Construction Management graduate from NYU, bringing valuable hands-on experience across India and the US.

Actively involved in project planning, execution, and quality management, focusing on integrating global best practices into Indian construction environments.
