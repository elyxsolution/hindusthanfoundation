About us

We build what you never see.

Pile foundation specialists in Bengaluru since 1998. Every tower, tech park and terminal we have worked on stands on foundations we bored, reinforced, concreted and tested.

Over 25 years of piling, from first rig to final load test.

Hindustan Foundation & Constructions is a specialist pile foundation contractor based in Bengaluru. We bore the hole, lower in the steel cage, fill it with concrete, and then prove it with a load test.

Our work runs from residential towers and villas to tech parks, industrial plants, defence infrastructure and airport structures, including Terminals 1 and 2 at Bangalore International Airport.

We own and operate our fleet of rotary rigs, cranes and excavators, so the people who plan a pile are the people who build it.
